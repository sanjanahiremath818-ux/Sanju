03 · Website improvement ideas

Useful basics. Memorable experiences.

These numbered features can make the existing expo website easier to use and more valuable for visitors and exhibitors.

01 · BASIC
🎟️

One-Minute Visitor Registration

Short form with name, mobile, email and visitor type. Send a confirmation and QR pass after connecting a registration service.

Benefit: Faster entry and better visitor records.
02 · BASIC
🧭

Interactive Stall Directory

Filter stalls by fabrics, machines, services, design or training. Add hall, stall number and directions.

Benefit: Visitors find relevant exhibitors quickly.
03 · BASIC
🏷️

Exhibitor Mini-Profiles

Each exhibitor gets a profile with logo, products, catalogue, contact details and enquiry button.

Benefit: More visibility before, during and after the expo.
04 · BASIC
📅

Live Programme & Reminders

Show fashion show timings, workshops and demonstrations. Let visitors add sessions to their calendar.

Benefit: Better planning and session attendance.
05 · INNOVATIVE
📍

QR-Powered Expo Map

Visitors scan a QR code at the entrance to open the floor map and locate a stall or facility.

Benefit: Less confusion and smoother movement.
06 · INNOVATIVE
🤝

Smart Business Matchmaking

Visitors select interests such as fabric sourcing or machinery. Suggest relevant exhibitors and meeting slots.

Benefit: More meaningful business conversations.
07 · INNOVATIVE
🧵

Save My Expo Trail

Visitors bookmark stalls and create a personal route such as “fabric suppliers → embroidery → machines”.

Benefit: A personalised visit and more stall discovery.
08 · INNOVATIVE
🎁

Digital Stamp & Rewards Trail

Scan participating stall QR codes to collect digital stamps and unlock a small reward or lucky-draw entry.

Benefit: Encourages visitors to explore more exhibitors.
09 · INNOVATIVE
📸

Social Photo Wall

Visitors share expo photos using an official hashtag. Display approved posts in a moderated gallery.

Benefit: Builds community and online promotion.
10 · INNOVATIVE
⭐

Feedback & Lead Capture

Quick visitor feedback and exhibitor lead forms with consent. Share a post-event summary with organisers.

Benefit: Measures satisfaction and helps exhibitors follow up.
11 · BASIC
🌐

Multi-language & Accessibility

Offer English, Marathi and Hindi, readable text, mobile-friendly pages and clear accessibility information.

Benefit: Makes the expo welcoming to a wider audience.
12 · INNOVATIVE
💬

Expo Help Assistant

A simple FAQ assistant answers questions about entry, venue, parking, timings, stalls and programme.

Benefit: Reduces repetitive enquiries and improves visitor support.
